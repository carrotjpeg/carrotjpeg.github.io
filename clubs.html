01. Engineering Solutions at Berkeley

Engineering Solutions at Berkeley is a student-run, hardware engineering consulting organization that comprises of students who create cost-efficient, reliable, and high-quality solutions for our clients.

Last semester, I worked on a closed-loop gas flow control system for corrosive environments built for a Bay Area stealth startup. We integrated industrial sensors and PID logic to achieve precise and reliable system performance.

This semester, my team is prototyping a Custom Parallelized Miniature Linear Array Detector for High-throughput Charge Detection Mass Spectrometer for Berkeley's QB3 Lab.

02. CALI

CALI is a flexible, multi-electrode neural wearable that continuously monitors brain activity in real time. The device is a thin flex PCB designed to adhere to the nape of the neck and is intended for clinical use, allowing psychiatrists to remotely track patients’ neural and physiological signals during daily life.

I am leading the microchip and sensor system design, spanning electrophysiology interfaces, signal acquisition, and validation. I work at the intersection of PCB design and AI/ML, ensuring low-noise, high-fidelity data for embedded machine-learning and scalable clinical deployment.
