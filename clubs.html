01. Engineering Solutions at Berkeley

Engineering Solutions at Berkeley is a student-run, non-profit hardware engineering consulting organization that comprises the brightest mechanical engineers, designers, and makers who create cost-efficient, reliable, and high-quality solutions for our clients.

Over the course of the semester, we work with companies to creatively improve workplace efficiency, solve independent engineering challenges, and prototype and test solutions.

This semester, I am working with a stealth startup in the Bay Area to build a closed-loop gas flow control system for corrosive environments by integrating industrial sensors, PCBAs, PLCs, and automated control architectures to achieve precise and reliable system performance.

02. CALI

CALI is a flexible, multi-electrode neural wearable that continuously monitors brain activity in real time. The device is a thin flex PCB designed to adhere to the nape of the neck and is intended for clinical use, allowing psychiatrists to remotely track patients’ neural and physiological signals during daily life.

I am leading the microchip and sensor system design, spanning electrophysiology interfaces, signal acquisition, and validation. I work at the intersection of PCB design and AI/ML, ensuring low-noise, high-fidelity data for embedded machine-learning and scalable clinical deployment.
