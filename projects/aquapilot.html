aquapilot
project page · spring 2025

AquaPilot is an AI-driven irrigation system I built with a team from robotics to optimize school garden watering. Inspired by WhyGreen, an environmental volunteering platform I co-founded, I wanted to take service further through hardware. We engineered a Raspberry Pi controller interfacing with capacitive soil moisture and DHT22 climate sensors, and trained supervised ML models on a UC Davis terrestrial plant database to predict watering schedules in Java, C++, and Python. Sensor telemetry drives solenoid valves in real time, reducing waste. The system is now operational in our school's gardens, and we are expanding across the Tustin Unified School District.
