cali neuropatch
project page · nov 2025 – may 2026

CALI is a flexible, multi-electrode neural wearable designed as a thin flex PCB that adheres to the nape of the neck, continuously monitoring brain activity so psychiatrists can remotely track patients' neural and physiological signals during daily life. As Lead Microchip Engineer, I led the PCB, microchip and sensor system design — electrophysiology interfaces, signal acquisition, and validation — and supported embedded ML readiness by ensuring low-noise, high-fidelity data for on-device inference. My scope covered signal-quality validation, sensor reliability, and noise & artifact analysis across motion, sweat, and EMI. As an upcoming startup, CALI is NDA-protected, so technical details remain confidential.
