Projects

01. MIT Beaverworks Brailliance

Brailliance is an affordable braille display. Translating text to ASCII, and converting ASCII to binary with an ASIC, the chip's data sends signals to actuators that move the braille pins, ultimately reducing the price of a portable braille reader by 90%, aiding visually-impaired people in their daily life.

02. WhyGreen

WhyGreen works to close the knowledge gap regarding environmentalism by providing a website where students can find volunteering opportunities using a map-based platform.

03. AquaPilot

AquaPilot is an AI-driven irrigation system I built with a team to optimize school garden watering. Using soil moisture and climate sensors connected to a Raspberry Pi, we trained supervised ML models on agricultural datasets to automate scheduling and control solenoid valves, reducing water waste through real-time prediction and feedback.

05. Inspirit AI

Developed a real-time video object detection model with confidence scoring for autonomous vehicles using YOLO, covering concepts like sliding windows, IoU, NMS, Darknet inputs, and demonstrating outputs and results from the machine learning model.
